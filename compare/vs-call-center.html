⏳

No Hold Music

Call centers queue callers when volume spikes — exactly when you can least afford it. TARA answers every line at once, so a Friday-night rush never turns into a busy signal.

Predictable Cost

Per-minute and per-agent billing punishes you for being busy. TARA runs on flat, usage-based pricing that scales with your revenue — not against it.

Outsourced agents read from a script and re-key orders by hand. TARA is wired into your POS with an always-current menu, so orders land accurately the first time.
