HeyTARA vs Slang AI

Slang AI is built around reservations and private events. HeyTARA is built around phone orders that land in your POS. Here's how they compare on pricing, integrations and what each one handles, using facts each company publishes.

Short Answer

Which One Fits Your Restaurant?

Slang AI calls itself “the AI Superhost for restaurants.” Its strengths are reservation management through OpenTable, SevenRooms, Resy and Yelp, private-event leads through Tripleseat and Perfect Venue, and broad language support. Its pricing page lists plans starting at $399 and $599 per location.

HeyTARA is built for restaurants where the phone is also an ordering channel. TARA takes takeout orders into Square, Olo/Omnivore or NCR Aloha, upsells on every order, texts payment links and handles catering and complaints, starting at $99/month with unlimited calls and no commissions.

A reservation-led fine dining room and a high-volume takeout brand need different things from the phone. Pick the tool built for the calls you actually get.

Common Questions

HeyTARA vs Slang AI FAQ

Slang AI focuses on reservations, private events and guest questions for full-service restaurants, with integrations for OpenTable, SevenRooms, Resy, Yelp, Tripleseat and Perfect Venue. HeyTARA focuses on phone orders that go straight into your POS (Square, Olo/Omnivore, NCR Aloha), plus FAQs, reservations, catering and complaints, starting at $99/month.

Slang's pricing page lists its Core plan starting at $399 per location and Premium starting at $599 per location, with Enterprise by quote. HeyTARA's Starter plan is $99/month and Main Course, which adds POS ordering, is $199/month, both with unlimited calls and no commissions.

The integrations Slang highlights on its website are reservation, review and private-event platforms: OpenTable, SevenRooms, Resy, Yelp, Tripleseat and Perfect Venue. We didn't find a POS ordering integration listed. If phone takeout orders matter to you, ask Slang directly. HeyTARA sends phone orders into Square, Olo/Omnivore and NCR Aloha on the Main Course plan.

If most of your calls are about reservations and private events and you run OpenTable, SevenRooms or Resy, Slang's reservation integrations are its core strength. If takeout, catering and phone orders drive a meaningful share of revenue, HeyTARA is built around getting those orders into your POS.

Slang lists bilingual support on Core and 97 languages on Premium. HeyTARA understands and responds to callers in Spanish on the Main Course plan.

Slang says it provides a new local or toll-free number and that keeping your existing number is something to discuss with its onboarding team. Ask HeyTARA on your demo how your current number would be set up for your locations.
