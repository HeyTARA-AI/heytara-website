Short Answer

Which One Fits Your Restaurant?

Both are built specifically for restaurants. Both answer unlimited calls at the same time, take takeout orders into your POS, handle reservations and catering, and transfer guests to your team when needed.

Loman lists more POS and reservation integrations today, including Toast, Clover, SpotOn, Shift4, OpenTable and Resy, and prices by quote.

HeyTARA publishes flat pricing starting at $99/month with unlimited calls and no commissions, lets you start with answering only and add ordering later, connects to Square, Olo/Omnivore and NCR Aloha, and goes past guest calls into complaint management, team member requests and help-desk routing for multi-unit groups.

HeyTARA vs Loman AI FAQ
