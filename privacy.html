Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. Here's exactly how HeyTARA collects, uses, and protects information.

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hey Tara LLC ("HeyTARA," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects information. It covers our website, the voice, text and email services we provide to restaurants and other hospitality businesses (our "customers"), and the guests who call or text those businesses (together, the "Services").

Called a restaurant and spoke with a virtual assistant? Start with section 1. It explains who answered, what was recorded, who can see it and how it's used. California residents can find our Notice at Collection in section 9.

1. When You Call a Business That Uses HeyTARA

Who answers the call

Many restaurants and hospitality businesses use HeyTARA to answer their phones. When you call one of them, your call may be answered by a virtual assistant (an AI voice agent) provided by Hey Tara LLC. We call it TARA, and some businesses use a voice tailored to their brand. The assistant answers live, in real time, on behalf of the business you called. It can answer questions, take orders, help with reservations, text you links and connect you to the business's team.

The business you called decides how the assistant is set up for its locations, and it is responsible for its own food, orders, prices, refunds and service. HeyTARA handles your call to provide our Services to that business, under our agreement with it. The business's own privacy policy also applies to how it uses your information.

What you'll hear at the start of the call

The assistant introduces itself, tells you it is a virtual assistant and says: “Calls are recorded and transcribed for quality assurance.” If you don't want your call recorded, you can hang up and contact the business another way, such as in person or through its website.

What we collect during a call

  • The audio of the call, which HeyTARA receives in real time so the assistant can understand you and respond.
  • A recording of the call and a written transcript of it.
  • Your phone number and call details: the number you called from, the business and location you called, and the date, time and length of the call.
  • Information you share during the call, which can include your name, phone number, email address, order details, pickup time, reservation details and anything else you say, such as a question, a complaint or a special request.
  • Information about how the call went, such as whether an order was placed, whether you were transferred to a team member, and labels our system adds automatically (for example, “catering” or “missing item”).
  • Text messages we send or receive for the business, such as ordering links, order confirmations and payment links, and any replies you send.

Please share only what's needed for your request. If you mention a food allergy or dietary need, it will appear in the transcript and may be added to your order so the business can prepare it correctly.

How calls are recorded, transcribed and stored

HeyTARA records the call and creates a written transcript using automated speech recognition. The recording, the transcript and the information collected during the call are stored with Amazon Web Services (AWS), our cloud hosting provider, and are encrypted in transit and at rest. To answer you in real time, the audio and text of the call are also processed by service providers that help us run the Services, such as telephony, text messaging, speech recognition, voice and AI language providers. We share with them only what they need to perform those services.

Who can see information about your call

  • The business you called. Depending on its plan and settings, the business's authorized team members can see your call in the HeyTARA dashboard, including the date, time and location, your phone number, the outcome, any order placed, the transcript, a summary and its labels, and the recording. The business can also download its reports.
  • The business's team, when your call is routed to them. If you are transferred, the team member may first hear a short summary of why you're calling. Complaint details, the location and the full transcript can be emailed to the business's team for follow-up. Catering and large-order requests can be sent to its catering team by phone or email, and employee or HR questions to its help desk (such as Zendesk). Orders are sent to the business's point-of-sale or online ordering system.
  • HeyTARA team members who need access to provide support, quality assurance (described below) and security.
  • Our service providers, as described above and in section 5.
  • Others only when required, such as to comply with the law or protect safety, as described in section 5.

We don't sell information about your call, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

What “quality assurance” means

HeyTARA and the business you called use recordings, transcripts and call information to:

  • Handle your request: place your order, answer your question, follow up on a complaint or connect you with the right person.
  • Check order accuracy: compare what you asked for with what was sent to the kitchen, and correct mistakes.
  • Troubleshoot misunderstandings: find where the assistant misheard, misunderstood or gave a wrong answer, and fix it.
  • Improve the calling experience: make the assistant's answers, menu knowledge, wording and transfers work better for callers.
  • Coach staff on effective wording, including upsells: help the business train its team members, and help us refine the assistant's wording, for example how add-ons and specials are offered.

Training our AI models

HeyTARA uses de-identified call data to train and improve the AI models that power the assistant. Before call data is used for training, we remove information that identifies you, such as your name, phone number and email address. We keep and use de-identified data only in de-identified form and do not attempt to re-identify it.

HeyTARA does not use your call to market to you. If you give a business your email address during a call, that business may send you news and offers under its own privacy policy, and you can unsubscribe from those emails at any time.

Paying for a phone order

HeyTARA does not ask callers to read card numbers aloud and does not process card payments. When a phone order needs to be paid for, we text you a secure payment link, and you enter your card details on a separate payment page run by a payment processor, not by HeyTARA. HeyTARA never sees, accesses, stores or processes your card number, expiration date or security code. We may receive confirmation of whether an order has been paid.

The assistant will never ask you to read your card number over the phone. If a caller claiming to be TARA does, hang up and contact the business directly.

Text messages

Depending on the business's setup, the assistant may text you during or after a call, for example an ordering link, an order confirmation or a payment link. These texts relate to your request. Message and data rates may apply. We do not share your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Talking to a person

You can ask for a person at any time, and the assistant will transfer you to the business's team. If the assistant can't continue a call for any reason, your call is sent to the business. The assistant is automated and can make mistakes, so please confirm anything important, such as allergy information, with the business's team.

Requests about your call

To request a copy of, correction to or deletion of your call recording, transcript or the information from your call, contact the business you called or email hello@heytara.ai. Please include the phone number you called from, the business and location you called, and the approximate date and time of the call. Because we hold this information for the business, we will usually work with the business to respond, and we may need to verify that the request comes from you. See section 8 for details.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few ways:

  • Information you provide. When you book a demo, contact us or sign up, you may give us your name, email address, phone number, business name, job title and details about your restaurant or hospitality operation.
  • Guest interaction data. When the Services handle calls, texts or emails for a customer, we process the content of those interactions, including recordings, transcripts, order details, reservation requests and contact information, as described in section 1.
  • Automatically collected data. Like most websites, we collect device information, browser type, IP address, pages visited and usage patterns through cookies and similar technologies.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain and improve the Services;
  • Answer calls, take orders, book reservations, route calls and send follow-ups on behalf of our customers;
  • Perform quality assurance as described in section 1;
  • Train and improve the AI models that power the Services, using de-identified data only;
  • Communicate with our customers and prospective customers about demos, support and product updates;
  • Analyze usage to improve performance, accuracy and reliability;
  • Detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse and security issues; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how the site is used and improve your experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect certain features. We do not sell your personal information.

Our website uses Google Analytics to measure traffic and HubSpot to understand visits and manage the contacts and inquiries we receive. HubSpot’s tracking code sets cookies and records the pages you view and your IP address. From the IP address, HubSpot may show us the name of the company whose network you are browsing from. If you have filled in a form or are already in contact with us, your visits may be linked to your contact record. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only as described in this policy:

  • With our customers. Information from a guest's call, text or email is shared with the business the guest contacted, as described in section 1.
  • With service providers that help us run the Services, including cloud hosting (Amazon Web Services), telephony and text messaging, speech recognition, voice and AI language providers, and analytics and customer relationship tools (Google Analytics and HubSpot). They receive only the information needed to perform their functions and are contractually required to protect it.
  • With systems our customers connect, such as point-of-sale, online ordering, reservation and help desk platforms, so orders, reservations and requests reach the business.
  • With payment processors, which handle card payments directly through their own payment pages. HeyTARA does not receive card details.
  • For legal and safety reasons, when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of HeyTARA, our customers, guests or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Call recordings, transcripts and other guest interaction records are kept while the business we serve has an active HeyTARA account and needs them to operate, unless the business sets a shorter period or asks us to delete them sooner. When a business stops using HeyTARA, we delete or de-identify its guest records within a reasonable time, unless we need to keep certain records longer to comply with the law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or enforce our agreements. Website and customer contact information is kept for as long as we have a business relationship with you or need it for the purposes above. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or de-identify it.

7. How We Protect Information

We use industry-standard administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest, to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction. Access to call recordings and transcripts is limited to authorized users at the business that received the call and to HeyTARA team members who need it to provide, support, secure or improve the Services. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

8. Your Rights & How to Make a Request

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know about and access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your personal information;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing; and
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

How to make a request. Email hello@heytara.ai, or, for a call to a restaurant or other business, contact that business directly. For call-related requests, include the phone number you called from, the business and location, and the approximate date and time of the call.

Verification. To protect your information, we will verify your identity before acting on a request, for example by confirming details of the call or contacting you at the phone number or email address in our records. You may use an authorized agent to make a request for you. We may ask the agent for proof of authorization and may ask you to confirm your identity directly.

When we act for a business. We handle guest call information on behalf of the business you called. If you send a request about a call to us, we may share it with that business and help it respond, or respond as the business directs.

Timing and limits. We will confirm that we've received your request and respond within the time required by applicable law (for California residents, within 45 days, which may be extended as the law allows). Some information may need to be kept, for example records of an order needed for tax purposes, to resolve a dispute or to comply with the law. If so, we'll tell you. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our response.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

9. California Notice at Collection & California Privacy Rights

This section applies to California residents and supplements the rest of this policy. When we process guest call information on behalf of a business, we act as that business's service provider, and the business's own notice also applies.

Categories of personal information we collect

  • Identifiers: name, phone number, email address and IP address.
  • Customer records information: name, phone number and email address you provide.
  • Commercial information: order details, items ordered, order totals, payment status (not card details), reservation details and requests.
  • Audio and electronic information: call audio, call recordings and text messages.
  • Other information from your interaction: call transcripts, summaries, outcomes and automatic call labels, and anything else you say during a call.
  • Internet or other electronic network activity: pages viewed and other website usage, collected through cookies.
  • Professional information: business name and job title of customers and prospective customers.

Sources: directly from you (including what you say during a call), from your device and phone carrier (such as caller ID and browser data), and from the businesses we serve and the systems they connect.

Sensitive personal information: we do not intend to collect sensitive personal information. If you share some during a call, such as a health detail like a food allergy, it is used only to handle your request. We do not collect payment card numbers, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would give you a right to limit its use under California law.

Why we collect it

To answer calls and handle requests on behalf of the businesses we serve, take and send orders, route calls, send texts, perform quality assurance as described in section 1, provide reporting to the businesses we serve, train and improve our AI models using de-identified data, operate and improve our website and the Services, communicate with customers and prospective customers, keep the Services secure and comply with the law.

How long we keep it

We keep each category of personal information for the periods described in section 6.

Sale and sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

De-identified information

Where we de-identify personal information, for example to train our AI models, we keep and use it only in de-identified form and do not attempt to re-identify it, except as permitted by law.

Your California rights

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to access, correct and delete it; to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do not sell or share it); to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not use it in ways that trigger this right); and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, follow the steps in section 8.

10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" above. Material changes will be communicated through the Services or by email where appropriate.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, or want to make a privacy request, contact Hey Tara LLC at hello@heytara.ai.
