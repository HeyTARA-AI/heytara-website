The Challenge

Why Phone Orders Are Hard on Aloha

Aloha runs some of the busiest multi-unit restaurants in the country. The phone is still the one channel that doesn't flow into it cleanly.

🔒

A Closed System

Aloha is harder to connect to than newer cloud POS systems, so many phone tools don't write orders into it. They text the guest an ordering link or send the order to the store to ring in by hand.

✍️

Orders Get Re-Keyed

When the phone isn't connected, someone writes the order down and rings it in again. That's where wrong modifiers, missed items and slow tickets come from.

🏢

Many Stores, Many Habits

Every location answers the phone differently, and corporate has no view into what happens on those calls or how many go unanswered.

⏱️

Peak Hits Twice

At the dinner rush, the person answering the phone is also running the register or expo. Calls ring out right when guests want to order.

🔌
