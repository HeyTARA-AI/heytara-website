The Challenge

Clover Runs the Counter. Who's Running the Phone?

Payments, the register and online orders all run through Clover. The phone still depends on whoever has a free hand.

🧑‍🍳

Everyone Is Already Busy

In a lean crew the person who answers the phone is also ringing up the counter and bagging orders. During the lunch rush, the phone loses.

📝

Notepad Orders

Orders written down by hand and keyed into Clover later are how a “no onions” or a missing side slips through.

🔗

Half-Finished Orders

Tools that only text a link hand the work back to the guest. Some finish the order. Some call the place down the street instead.

💳

Card Numbers Out Loud

Taking a card number by phone at a loud counter is slow, error-prone and not something you want staff writing down.

Before You Go Live

Get Your Clover Menu Phone-Ready

1

Names Guests Say

TARA reads your menu out loud, so “Chicken Caesar Wrap” works better than “CHX CSR WRP.” We'll flag any names that need a tweak.

2

Clean Modifier Groups

Clear group names, required choices like size marked as required, and no duplicates. TARA asks for what's required and offers what's optional.

3

Prices on Add-Ons

Put prices on modifiers like extra cheese or a size upgrade in Clover, so every phone order totals correctly.

4

Hide What You Don't Sell To-Go

Dine-in-only items, open items and test items shouldn't be offered by phone. We set up which items TARA offers callers.
